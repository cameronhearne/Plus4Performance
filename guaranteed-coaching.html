FAQ

Before you apply.

How does the guarantee actually work?

Simple. Hit 90%+ compliance across training, nutrition, steps and weigh-ins for the full 16 weeks. If you do that and still don't hit your agreed result, you get every penny back. Full terms are confirmed on your consultation call before you start, no surprises later.

Why should I pay this when I could hire any PT?

Because most PTs are guessing. We're not. This is built on weekly reviews, form correction on camera, and a plan that actually adjusts based on what's working for you. You're not paying for a generic programme with your name on it. You're paying for someone watching your progress every single week and being accountable for the result.

How much time do I actually get with you?

Real access, not a templated app experience. Weekly video check-ins, monthly live calls, and direct messaging when you need it. This isn't a subscription you log into and hope for the best. You're working with a coach who's actually looking at your training every week.

What if I'm away for work or travelling a lot?

Doesn't matter. The programme is built around your life, not the other way round. Training and nutrition plans adapt to wherever you are, and check-ins happen remotely either way. If you're serious about the result, travel isn't an excuse we accept, and it's not one you'll need.

What happens after I apply?

You'll be invited to a short consultation call. We confirm it's the right fit, agree your result, and go through the guarantee terms properly before anything starts. No pressure, no hard sell, just making sure this is right before you commit.
