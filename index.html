Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as my "agreed result"?

It's set individually with your coach in week one, based on your starting point and what's realistic and healthy to achieve in 16 weeks — not a generic number picked for you.

What happens if I miss a check-in?

Life happens — one or two missed weeks won't disqualify you from the guarantee, which requires at least 15 of 16 submitted. Missed check-ins do affect your compliance score, so it's worth flagging changes to your coach early.

How is the 90% compliance score calculated?

It's a weighted scorecard covering training, nutrition, steps/cardio and weigh-ins/check-ins across the full 16 weeks — not just one part of the plan. Your coach can show you exactly where you stand at any point.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes — £899 upfront, or 4 monthly payments of £250.

What if I need to pause for injury or travel?

Talk to your coach as early as possible. Genuine circumstances are handled case by case — the goal is to get you to a fair result, not catch you out on a technicality.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Your programme and your agreed result are built around your current experience level, not a one-size-fits-all standard.
