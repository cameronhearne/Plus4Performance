FAQ

Before you book.

How do check-ins work?

Once a week, at a time we agree with you, you send us your check-in through the app: your weight, progress photos, how training's gone and how you've got on with your food. We go through it properly and adjust your plan for the week ahead.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. It's monthly, there's no contract. Just let us know before your next payment and it won't renew.

Do I need a gym membership?

It works best with gym access, but we coach people who train at home too. Tell us what kit you've got on the call and we'll build your plan around it.

What if I'm a beginner?

That's fine. Your plan is built around where you're at, not where we are. We'll teach you the basics, get your form right from day one and build from there. Everyone starts somewhere.

What's the difference between this and Guaranteed Coaching?

Online Coaching is monthly. You get your plan, weekly check-ins and direct access to us, and you can stop whenever you want. Guaranteed Coaching is a 16-week programme with twice-weekly check-ins and a goal we agree on at the start. Do your part, and if you don't hit it, you get your money back. Got a specific goal and a deadline? Go guaranteed. Want ongoing coaching? This is the one.
