Shipping & Returns

Shipping & returns.

Everything made to order gets printed once you buy it, so a few things work differently to a normal shop.

Delivery

  • We deliver to UK addresses only.
  • UK delivery: £2.99 for a single tee, £3.99 for everything else. Free on orders over £50.
  • Every item is made to order for you by our production partner. Allow 2 to 4 days for production. Once shipped, around 60% of orders arrive within a week and 95% within 13 days. We will always deliver within 30 days of your order.
  • If you need an update on your order, reply to your order confirmation email or contact us at cameron@plus4performance.com.
  • If your order has not arrived within 30 days of ordering, contact us and we will send a replacement or give you a full refund.

Changed your mind?

  • You can cancel your order at any time up to 14 days after the day you receive it. You don't need to give a reason.
  • To cancel, email cameron@plus4performance.com with your order reference within that 14 days, then send the item back to us within 14 days of telling us.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition. We may reduce your refund if an item has been worn or damaged beyond what's needed to check it.
  • You pay the cost of returning the item. We recommend a tracked service, as we can't refund items that don't reach us.
  • We will refund you to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the item back.
  • Return to: 77 Smeath Road, Underwood, NG16 5GU

Faulty, damaged or wrong item?

  • If your item arrives faulty, damaged, misprinted or isn't what you ordered, email cameron@plus4performance.com within 30 days of delivery with your order reference and a photo.
  • We will send a replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer. If we need the item back, we will cover the return cost.
  • This does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Sizing

Check our Size Guide before ordering. Wrong size orders are treated as a change of mind return.

Questions about your order or need to return something? Email us at cameron@plus4performance.com
